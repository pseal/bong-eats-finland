Our Story

Where Bengal
meets Finland

Bong Eats Finland was born from a simple longing — the deep, comforting flavors of Bengali home cooking, served thousands of miles from where it all began.

Every dish we craft is a love letter to the culinary traditions of Bengal. From the slow-simmered curries perfumed with mustard oil to the delicate sweetness of our handmade desserts, we bring authenticity to every plate.

Using locally sourced Finnish ingredients wherever possible, combined with traditional Bengali spice blends, we create a bridge between two beautiful cultures — one plate at a time.
